    SHIP'S MANUAL

    Walk — click anywhere on the floor (or use WASD / arrow keys).
    Interact — click glowing consoles, plants, crew, creatures and objects. The tooltip tells you what you're pointing at.
    Items — click an item in your inventory to examine it, use it on something, eat it or feed it to Comet. Right-click or Esc to stop using an item.
    Camera — drag to look, scroll to zoom, V toggles Behind / First-Person view.
    Full screen — the Full Screen button (top right, or on the title screen) or F. Press it again, or Esc, to leave.
    Voices — the 🗣 Voices button turns speech on or off and lets you choose and test a voice for each character. It also has Auto-Play, the narrator and the conversation camera.
    HALO Scan — press Tab or 📡 Scan to label everything you can use in the room. On a phone, tap a label to use it, or press and hold on anything to see its name.
    Dialogue log — press L or 📜 Log to read back (and hear again) everything that's been said.
    Choices & keypads — press 1, 2, 3… to pick an answer, and type codes straight on your keyboard.

    Comet & AVE points — AVE stands for Animal Valor & Expertise. Comet spends them on her skills: Sniff (1) finds hidden things and makes friends, Bark (1) scares or lures creatures, Dig (2) digs up buried things, Fetch (2) reaches tight spots and tugs things free. Refill AVE by petting her (click Comet), feeding her carrots or biscuits, and at Save Beacons.

    Fetch! — take the squeaky ball from Comet's bed on the Bridge, pick it in your inventory and click the floor to throw it.
    Lost treasures — the impact scattered eight keepsakes around the ship. When there's nothing else to find, Comet's Sniff leads you to one with glowing paw prints; then Dig or Fetch brings it back.

    Save Beacons — the glowing cyan pylons. Click one to save. If you fall, you return to your last save.

    Stuck? Press 💡 Hint at the top of the screen. Each press of Clearer hint tells you a bit more, right up to the exact answer. You can also talk to the crew, read the Captain's log, and ask Comet to sniff around.

    VOICES

    These voices come from your own browser and device, so what's on offer varies. The most natural-sounding are Microsoft Edge's "Natural" voices and Chrome's "Google" voices.
